'''Prem Singh Yadav''' is an Indian scientist whose research focuses on buffalo cloning<ref name="src-d03b0f5819">{{cite web|url=https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/india/cibr-scientists-clone-7-elite-m-29-buffaloes-re-clone-one-5561561.html|title=CIBR scientists clone 7 elite M-29 buffaloes, re-clone one|website=Moneycontrol|date=2020-07-17}}</ref>.
In 2020, Moneycontrol identified Yadav as head of the CIRB cloning team<ref name="src-d03b0f5819" />.

==Education==
Yadav is from Village Nimoth in Rewari district, Haryana<ref name="src-30cdb84a23">{{cite web|url=https://www.amarujala.com/haryana/rewari/principal-scientist-dr-prem-singh-retired-rewari-news-c-198-1-rew1001-218880-2025-05-03|title=प्रधान वैज्ञानिक डॉ. प्रेम सिंह हुए सेवानिवृत्त|website=Amar Ujala|date=3 May 2025}}</ref>. Born on 10 April 1963<ref name="src-e73557644a">{{cite web|url=https://cirb.res.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Staff-Remuneration-List-Hisar-2023.pdf|title=Scientists Remunerations|website=ICAR-Central Institute for Research on Buffaloes|date=2023}}</ref>. A publisher biography for Yadav's book states that he graduated from the College of Agriculture, HAU, in 1985 and obtained an M.Sc. in 1987 and Ph.D. in 1991 from the Department of Animal Production Physiology, College of Animal Sciences, CCSHAU<ref name="src-2d912bc8ca">{{cite web|url=https://www.satishserial.com/book/8189304801/reproductive-biotechnology-in-buffalo|title=Reproductive Biotechnology In Buffalo|website=satishserial.com}}</ref>.

==Career==
The 2023 ICAR-CIRB staff list recorded Yadav as a principal scientist with a joining date of 12 April 1993<ref name="src-e73557644a" />. Yadav joined ICAR as a Scientist in 1993, was selected as Senior Scientist in 2000, and promoted to Principal Scientist in 2008 at ICAR-CIRB Hisar<ref name="src-2d912bc8ca" />. As a senior scientist at ICAR-CIRB, Yadav began a 12-month Department of Biotechnology Overseas Associateship in Neustadt, Germany, on 15 November 2003<ref name="src-75cd616383">{{cite web|url=https://icar.org.in/sites/default/files/inline-files/PARTNERSHIP-AND-LINKAGES-02_0.pdf|title=ICAR Partnership and Linkages: Overseas training during 2003–04|website=Indian Council of Agricultural Research|date=2003}}</ref>. Yadav's research stay at the Institute for Animal Breeding (FAL), Mariensee, Germany, with Heiner Niemann's group produced the 2005 paper 'Bovine ICM derived cells express the Oct4 ortholog' in Molecular Reproduction and Development<ref name="src-0c979bd135">{{cite web|url=https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15973686/|title=Bovine ICM derived cells express the Oct4 ortholog|website=PubMed}}</ref>. An archived official ICAR-CIRB profile records that Yadav received DAAD Research support in 2010<ref name="src-87ac8ccc96">{{cite web|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20210723192859/https://cirb.icar.gov.in/ps-yadav/|title=Dr. Prem Singh Yadav|website=ICAR-Central Institute for Research on Buffaloes (archived official profile)|date=23 July 2021}}</ref>. ICAR-CIRB’s 2010–11 annual report records Yadav’s research stay at Farm Animal Genetics, Germany, from 28 December 2010 to 10 February 2011<ref name="src-4fdcf94f36">{{cite web|url=https://cirb.res.in/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/annualreport2010-2011.pdf|title=ICAR-Central Institute for Research on Buffaloes Annual Report 2010–11|website=ICAR-Central Institute for Research on Buffaloes|date=2010–11}}</ref>. In 2018, The Tribune identified Yadav as a principal scientist and project head at ICAR-CIRB<ref name="src-12f91675f5">{{cite web|url=https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/archive/haryana/cloned-bull-s-semen-used-to-produce-20-calves-in-8-months-698521/|title=Cloned bull's semen used to produce 20 calves in 8 months|website=The Tribune|date=14 December 2018}}</ref>. An ICAR news release named Yadav as principal investigator of the NASF-funded buffalo cloning project<ref name="src-9a6bdc8d41">{{cite web|url=https://icar.org.in/en/node/12458|title=ICAR-CIRB makes commendable achievement in Buffalo Cloning by producing 7 clones from a Single Breeding Bull|website=Indian Council of Agricultural Research|date=16 July 2020}}</ref>. An archived ICAR-CIRB profile lists Yadav as Principal Scientist and Head of the Division of Animal Physiology and Reproduction<ref name="src-87ac8ccc96" />. In 2022, a PTI report described him as principal investigator of the Hisar Gaurav cloning project<ref name="src-5371d6d304">{{cite web|url=https://theprint.in/india/hisar-gaurav-first-cloned-calf-born-at-icar-cirb-turns-7/1260625/|title='Hisar Gaurav', first cloned calf born at ICAR-CIRB, turns 7|website=theprint.in}}</ref>. ICAR-CIRB's 2024 annual report listed Yadav as a principal scientist<ref name="src-aeb6d915a9">{{cite web|url=https://cirb.res.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/CIRB_AR_2024.pdf|title=ICAR-Central Institute for Research on Buffaloes Annual Report 2024|website=ICAR-Central Institute for Research on Buffaloes|date=2024}}</ref>. Yadav retired as an ICAR-CIRB principal scientist in 2025<ref name="src-30cdb84a23" />.

ICAR reported that a team led by Yadav produced the cloned Murrah bull Hisar Gaurav in 2015<ref name="src-443a3f8b23">{{cite web|url=https://icar.gov.in/node/4887|title=ICAR-CIRB become India’s second center to produce cloned buffalo {{!}} ICAR|website=icar.gov.in}}</ref>. NDTV reported that the CIRB team headed by Yadav produced the cloned Assamese buffalo Sach-Gaurav in December 2017<ref name="src-b4ab04664d">{{cite web|url=https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/indias-first-cloned-assamese-buffalo-born-1824454|title=India’s First Cloned Assamese Buffalo Born|website=NDTV|date=15 March 2018}}</ref>. ICAR-CIRB reported that Yadav was the team leader for Hisar Gaurav, which weighed 270 kg at its first birthday<ref name="src-3fcefbb56a">{{cite web|url=https://cirb.res.in/news/icar-cirb-celebrates-first-birthday-of-cloned-calf-hisar-gaurav/|title=ICAR-CIRB celebrates first birthday of cloned calf, Hisar-Gaurav|website=ICAR-Central Institute for Research on Buffaloes|date=2017}}</ref>. The Times of India reported that the Hisar Gaurav cloning team worked under Yadav's leadership<ref name="src-89f4cef816">{{cite web|url=https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/gurgaon/clone-murrah-buffalo-hisar-gaurav-turns-three-has-produced-20-male-and-female-calves/articleshow/67084199.cms|title=1st clone male Murrah buffalo turns 3|website=The Times of India|date=2018-12-14}}</ref>. Moneycontrol reported in 2020 that the CIRB team had cloned seven M-29 buffaloes and recloned Hisar Gaurav<ref name="src-d03b0f5819" />. Zee News reported that CIRB Hisar produced eight clones, seven from the M-29 bull and a re-clone of Hisar-Gaurav, born between October 2019 and January 2020, under Dr Prem Singh Yadav as head of the cloning team<ref name="src-6ee08c1bb8">{{cite web|url=https://zeenews.india.com/india/cibr-hisar-achieves-a-milestone-clones-8-calves-to-improve-buffalo-breed-2296320.html|title=CIBR Hisar achieves a milestone, clones 8 calves to improve buffalo breeding|website=Zee News|date=2020}}</ref>. ICAR-CIRB reported in July 2020 that its team, including Yadav, produced seven clones of the superior breeding bull M-29 and a re-cloned calf of Hisar Gaurav, born between October 2019 and January 2020<ref name="src-757d58e8aa">{{cite web|url=https://cirb.res.in/news/icar-cirb-makes-commendable-achievement-in-buffalo-cloning-by-producing-sev|title=ICAR-CIRB makes commendable achievement in buffalo cloning by producing seven clones from a single breeding bull|website=ICAR-Central Institute for Research on Buffaloes|date=2020-07-30}}</ref>. Dharmakshethra reported that a CIRB team including Yadav found the semen and fertility profiles of cloned bulls to be normal, in a PLOS ONE study<ref name="src-b58c5cbf8c">{{cite web|url=https://dharmakshethra.com/researchers-find-semen-and-fertility-profiles-of-cloned-bulls-normal-may-|title=Researchers Find Semen and Fertility Profiles of Cloned Bulls Normal|website=Dharmakshethra|date=2020}}</ref>. Business Standard reported in 2021 that Yadav described efforts to demonstrate cloning’s use in multiplying proven buffalo bulls for breeding<ref name="src-1a851e9be8">{{cite web|url=https://www.business-standard.com/article/opinion/cloning-overcomes-prejudices-121072600018_1.html|title=Cloning overcomes prejudices|website=Business Standard|date=26 July 2021}}</ref>. ICAR-CIRB reported that its scientists produced Veer Gaurav, a male buffalo calf born through ovum pick-up and in-vitro embryo production using semen of the cloned bull Hisar Gaurav, with a team that included Yadav<ref name="src-098320bb8e">{{cite web|url=https://cirb.res.in/news/icar-cirb-produces-veer-gaurav-through-opu-ivep-technology/|title=ICAR-CIRB produces Veer Gaurav through OPU-IVEP technology|website=ICAR-Central Institute for Research on Buffaloes|date=2023-01-13}}</ref>.
